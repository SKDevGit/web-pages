Cost, choice and where to start

3 exercises

Three practical questions come up in every adoption discussion: which model, at what cost, and for which job first. This page gives you a simple way to think about each.

Where to start

Good first projects have high volume, low risk, and answers that are easy to check. Switch each criterion on or off to see how the use cases rank.

Starting values are suggestions. Change them to match your own business.

What to remember

Match the model to the job, not to the headline. Start with a task that is frequent, forgiving and easy to review, learn from it, and then move to harder work.
