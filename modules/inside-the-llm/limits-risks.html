Limits and risks

4 exercises

A model produces likely-sounding text. That is why it is useful, and also why it can be wrong with great confidence. Knowing where it fails is what lets you use it safely at work.

By the end you canspot an unsupported claim, explain where bias comes from, say what should never go into a public tool, and match each risk to a safeguard.

Many companies license a private, enterprise version with different terms. Always follow your firm’s rules about which tool to use.

What to remember

A model is a fast, fluent assistant, not a source of record. Give it the sources, keep a person responsible for anything that matters, and keep sensitive data out of tools that are not approved for it.
