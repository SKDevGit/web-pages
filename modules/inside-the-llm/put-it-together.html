What this means for you

Knowledge has a date. What the model learned stops at its training cutoff. Anything current has to arrive in the prompt.

Answers vary. Sampling means two runs can differ. Test with many runs, and lower the temperature when you need consistency.

Tokens are the meter. Cost, speed, and the size of the brief you can supply are all measured in tokens.

Context is your lever. You can’t retrain a model in a meeting, but you can change what it reads before it answers.
