Context and attention

3 exercises

Everything a model knows about your situation arrives in the prompt. Change that text and the answer changes, even if your question stays word for word the same. Inside the model, a mechanism called attention decides which parts of the text matter for each word it writes.

By the end you canshow how a different brief changes an answer, and explain in one sentence what attention does.

Step 2 of 3

Attention: which words matter

When the model reads a word, it looks back at earlier words and gives each a weight. Click any word to see where it looks. Then swap the last word of the sentence and notice what changes.

Last word:

Weights are hand-set to illustrate the idea. Real models learn them, run many patterns in parallel, and stack them in many layers. A word can only look at words before it, so later words are dimmed.

Meaning as numbers

Before attention can work, each token becomes a list of numbers called an embedding. Words used in similar ways get nearby numbers. Click a word on the map to see its closest neighbors.
