Making it fit your business

4 exercises

A general model knows nothing about your company. There are three ways to close that gap, and they differ a lot in cost and effort: write a better prompt, let the model look things up in your documents, or train it further on your examples.

By the end you candescribe prompting, retrieval and fine-tuning, and pick the right one for a given business need.

Let it look things up (retrieval)

Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, searches your own documents first, then adds the best passages to the prompt. Pick a question, or type your own, and see which passages are found.

Train it on your examples (fine-tuning)

Fine-tuning continues training on examples you supply, so the parameters themselves change. It is best for a consistent style or format. Drag to add examples of your company’s own replies.

What to remember

Start with the cheapest option and move up only if it is not enough. Prompts first, retrieval when the model needs your facts, fine-tuning when you need a consistent behavior that prompts cannot give you.
