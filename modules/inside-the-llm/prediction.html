See what a model produces

A language model doesn’t produce one answer. It produces odds for many possible endings. These percentages are illustrative, set by hand for this exercise.

Click Reveal the odds to see them. Then click Draw a random ending a few times: each click picks one ending according to those odds, so you will often get a different one.
