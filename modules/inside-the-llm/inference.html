How inference works

6 steps

Inference is what happens each time you send a model a prompt. The learned parameters are already fixed. The model reads your text, works out odds for the next word, picks one, and goes around again. You’ll follow one prompt through every step, from receiving it to picking the next word.

By the end you canlist the steps from prompt to next word, explain why a model can give different answers to the same prompt, and say which part of the process you control.

The big picture

This is what happens after the model is finished and packaged into a product. The parameters are frozen, so nothing here changes the model. The steps below follow the highlighted stages. Click any box for detail.

Step 1 of 6

Receive the prompt

The model is handed text. That is its entire view of the world: your prompt, plus any words it has already written. Choose the prompt you’ll follow through the rest of this page.

Step 2 of 6

Split into tokens

The text is cut into small pieces called tokens. Common words are usually one token. Rarer words are cut into several pieces.

Step 3 of 6

Turn tokens into numbers

Each token is swapped for an ID number from the model’s vocabulary. The model’s calculations only work on numbers, so from here on it never sees words again.

Step 4 of 6

Look at the context

Next the model decides which tokens matter for the next word. This is called attention. The tiny model here only looks at the last token. Large models weigh the whole prompt, as you’ll see in part 5.

Step 5 of 6

Score every next word

Using what training taught it, the model gives every word in its vocabulary a percentage chance of coming next. These are the numbers you shaped on the training page.

Step 6 of 6

Pick the next word

The model doesn’t always take the top word. It rolls weighted dice, so likelier words win more often. Temperature controls how adventurous the roll is: at 0 it always takes the top word. The picked word is added to the prompt, and the model goes around again.

Why the answers differ

Because of the dice in step 6, the same prompt can produce different replies. Run it ten times at the temperature you chose and see how much they vary.
